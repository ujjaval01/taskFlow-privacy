Privacy Policy

Last Updated • May 15, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to the Todo List application. We believe your productivity data should be yours alone. This policy outlines our commitment to transparency and data minimalist principles.

Data Collection

Our architecture is built on a "Privacy by Design" foundation. We intentionally collect zero personal data.

  • No Registration Required
  • No Email or Phone Number
  • No Tracking or Analytics
  • No Third-party Scripts

Local Sovereignty

Your tasks, notes, and preferences never leave your device. We utilize Local Storage and IndexedDB to keep your data resident on your hardware.

Connectivity

While the app may use internet access for periodic updates or optional synchronization, core functionality remains fully operational offline.

Children's Safety

As we do not collect any personal information, our application is safe for users of all ages, fully compliant with digital privacy standards.

Questions?

Our privacy team is here to help if you have any questions about our data practices.
